Atari 5200 SuperSystem core for the Analogue Pocket, ported from the MiSTer Atari 5200 core.

**Install:** unzip onto the root of your Pocket's SD card. Then put the 2048 byte Atari 5200 BIOS in `Assets/5200/common/` named `5200.rom` (not included), and your games (`.a52`, `.bin`, `.rom`, `.car`) in the same folder.

**Controls:** D-pad is the joystick, A is fire 1, B is fire 2, Start is Start and a tap of Select pauses. X/Y are keypad 1/2 and L/R are keypad * and #. Hold Select for keypad 3 to 0 (X, Y, A, B, Up, Right, Down, Left) and Select+Start for the Reset key.

**Supported carts:** 4K, 8K, 16K (one chip and two chip), 32K, 40K Bounty Bob, 64K and 128K super carts, and `.car` files.
